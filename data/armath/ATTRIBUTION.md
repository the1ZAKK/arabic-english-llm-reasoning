## Separate ArabicPRM-T contributions

ArMATH's original problems, equations, segmentation, tags and folds remain the
upstream authors' work and retain their original bytes, including known concerns.
ArabicPRM-T adds acquisition hashes, read-only checks, mathematical/semantic
screening, grouping analysis and research documentation. These contributions do
not confer original-Arabic authorship on translated examples, supply independent
human PRM step labels, correct source errors, or complete a validated benchmark.
