A non-pending export requires an actual personal-review attestation, supplied identity, rationale and the browser's actual UTC clock assertion. Loading a saved record never overwrites it and clears the attestation before a final re-export. Identity, clock, qualifications and independence are assertions, not authenticated facts. AI must never complete this as human review.
