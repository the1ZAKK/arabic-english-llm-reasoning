This brief is an AI-assisted source audit for the qualified **design** reviewer. It is not a completed human decision. It contains source concerns and must stay separate from the neutral, blinded outcome-review package. Start with the [unchanged reviewer ZIP and instructions](DIAGNOSTIC24_DESIGN_REVIEW_V2_1.md) and [pending checklist](DIAGNOSTIC24_HUMAN_DECISION_CHECKLIST_V2_2.md). The source-only replay reconstructed all 12 job contexts and 24 payload hashes from the frozen queue. These are five problem fields and seven native positive step fields, all in train. No negative or masked step is tested directly. The purposive design has seven problem groups, of which the circle and base groups contribute five and two slots respectively. The arms compare complete prompt policies (examples, wording, context, glossary and length differ), so this is not a glossary-only causal test or a population quality estimate. Six pairs dispatch the control first and six dispatch the contextual policy first. This balances which policy runs first but does not remove clustering, selection from unresolved slots, or previously observed failure exposure.
