# Facilitator key: invented bilingual mathematical fidelity practice

Share the [practice sheet](BILINGUAL_MATH_CALIBRATION_PRACTICE_V2_4.md) alone first. Keep this key private to the facilitator until each person has recorded independent practice reasoning. Do not send the real diagnostic design, prompts, known pilot concerns or arm mapping with the sheet. The key describes intended contrasts, not actual human judgments.

| ID | Intended contrast | What to discuss |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | A faithful; B translation error. | Universal `for every` becomes existential `for some`. This is a substantive quantifier/scope change. |
| Q2 | B faithful; A translation error. | A permits 4; English excludes it. The endpoint is the evidence, not the use of a particular Arabic word. |
| Q3 | A faithful; B translation error. | The literal 2.5 survives, but kilograms become grams. Arithmetic fidelity includes units and scale; severity depends on the downstream use. |
| Q4 | B faithful; A translation error. The English source is flawed in both comparisons. | 21 = 3 × 7. A repairs the mathematics and changes the original claim; B preserves it. Record source correctness independently of Arabic fidelity. |
| Q5 | A faithful; B translation error, ordinarily minor. | The feminine predicate disagrees with the masculine dual subject; equality is still recoverable. Do not call a stylistic variant an error. |
| Q6 | B faithful; A translation error. | The extra equality is unsupported and contradicts the inequality: x = 5 would give 8 > 8. Discuss severity in relation to the inference being evaluated. |
| Q7 | Unavailable for semantic judgment. | Retain the charged slot, record zero usable **recorded** response, and keep fidelity pending. Do not infer the model’s unseen behavior or a human translation error. |
| Q8 | Preserve uncertainty about the context-sensitive interpretation. | «مطابق» can be legitimate in a geometric context. A fragment-level plausibility observation does not settle terminology without the objects and local definition. Don't manufacture a severity or missing context. |

Ask each person to explain a boundary case in their own words, especially Q4 and Q7. If assessors confuse source truth with fidelity or missingness with semantic error, discuss fresh invented examples and repeat practice before exposing real outputs. Record calibration completion and any rubric clarifications privately. There is no mechanical passing score. Practice is separate from the real design decision, evidence seals and independent adjudication.
