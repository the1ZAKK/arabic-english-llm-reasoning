# Compact ArMATH problem-quality review

Work from the original `question` and `equation`, not only `segmented` text or AI
notes. The pinned source supplies no separate numeric answer or reasoning steps.
Read the exact source permalink; preserve the original even if it is wrong.

Judge each criterion as ACCEPT / REVISE / REJECT / UNCERTAIN, with specific evidence:

1. **Arabic clarity/grammar:** complete question, referents, operation scope and
   grammatical agreement; record ambiguity instead of choosing a convenient reading.
2. **Terminology/entities:** operation, radius/diameter, length/area/volume and
   mathematical relation mean what the Arabic says.
3. **Equation fidelity:** derive the relationship from the question independently;
   matching an equation to itself does not verify the wording.
4. **Quantities/units/domain:** numbers/fractions/percentages, conversions,
   nonnegative/discrete counts, integer affordability, bounds and rounding are valid.
   Negative arithmetic or fractional money is not automatically erroneous.
5. **Completeness/uniqueness:** enough information, appropriate assumptions, all
   requested quantities, unique intended outcome or explicitly recorded alternatives.
6. **Topic/difficulty:** actual mathematical task and reading burden; keyword and
   operator counts are signals, not qualified human labels.
7. **Provenance/use:** source `novel` declaration versus confirmed item origin,
   source-book lineage, applicable permission and potential family/training overlap.

Keep source error, equation annotation error, Arabic ambiguity/grammar, unit/domain/
rounding, duplicate/family, provenance/rights and parser-limit categories separate.
Suggested revisions are separate proposals, never edits to the immutable source.
UNCERTAIN remains unresolved. This development sample is purposive, not a semantic
accuracy estimate. Do not report agreement as accuracy.

Each assessor works independently in a separate CSV. Record actual reviewer type,
identity/reference, qualifications/independence evidence, decision, explanation and
date. Student review is **developer** review; an assessor cannot independently
label their own trace. No identities, decisions or dates are prefilled. Preserve and
hash/seal both initial CSVs before comparing them or reading AI triage notes.
A separate qualified adjudicator records disagreements, source A/B row hashes and
the actual outcome; disagreement never automatically becomes approval.

These forms assess problem quality only. Future original-Arabic solution traces
need separate step and final-answer judgments by qualified Arabic-speaking humans.
No English PRM800K labels, source equation values or AI triage become native human
step labels. Rights and independent human requirements still block corpus/test
eligibility; no model operation is authorized by completing a worksheet.
