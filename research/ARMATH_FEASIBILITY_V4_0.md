500 held-out items. A future family policy needs item lineage, expert review of
cross-group neighbors and a separately frozen contamination audit.

## Human work and feasibility decision

The delivered packet provides exact original Arabic questions, source pointers,
equations, source-equation calculations, separately question-derived references,
uncertainties and quality flags. `START_HERE.html` is the entry point. The first-pass
question page omits calculated answers and source equations; coordinator references
are in a different file. Separation is **procedural**, not enforced blinding:
source links and other packet files can reveal equations/calculations.

The student has a separate developer worksheet. A/B each require a genuine qualified
Arabic-speaking mathematics assessor, their own independently derived answers,
explanations, uncertainty and actual identity/qualification/independence/date evidence.
Preserve initial submissions before sharing AI calculations or other judgments.
A distinct qualified adjudicator handles disagreements, retaining A/B originals
and row hashes. No assessor may supply an independent label for their own trace.
All people/judgment/date fields are currently blank; all human counts remain **0**.

Start with the fixed first ten, then the remaining 90 after measuring effort and
resolving unclear instructions without overwriting initial assessments. Existing
v3.1 assumptions yield **25.1–62.8 combined person-hours** for 100 coordinator
screens, 200 independent problem/reference checks, assumed adjudication and 15%
contingency. These are **not observed timings** and exclude recruitment, lineage/
permission investigation, transcription, original Arabic trace creation and all
step/final annotation. The existing v3.2 timing headers are reused; record actual
work and rejected/uncertain items, never hypothetical sessions as observations.

**Decision:** ready for a human **problem-quality feasibility review**. ArMATH's
3,533 source-declared native candidates offer sufficient headcount to investigate
100, 250 or 500, but headcount is not suitable/diverse independent yield. The current
100 spans many formula templates while remaining predominantly elementary
arithmetic/applied contexts and geometry. It does not establish coverage of the
project domains (`arithmetic_number_theory`, `algebra`, `geometry`,
`probability_combinatorics`, `applied_word_problems`); the prior limited keyword
screen found no probability/combinatorics signals. Supplementary native sources,
human topic/difficulty/provenance judgments, real effort and defensible family
separation are needed before promising a 250/500-problem benchmark.

Original-source redistribution permission is documented separately from the
historical audit snapshot. A future cleaned/independently annotated release remains
a distinct deliverable. **New Arabic traces, human step labels, eligible PRM
benchmark items and model observations remain zero.** No inference, PRM scoring,
training, budget activation, scheduled execution, protected-history or main change
occurs. CI verifies software guards and reproducibility, not mathematical/linguistic
accuracy, actual people or completed human QC.
