# Research decisions

1. Preserve frozen English, mathematical literals, recorded ratings, checkpoints and accepted Arabic exactly. Flag source reasoning errors separately; faithful translation must not silently correct them.
2. Count slots, complete fields and complete trajectories separately. Twenty accepted fragments cannot be called twenty complete fields or records without reconstruction.
3. Treat current strict-check failures in historical drafts as revalidation work. Do not rewrite original drafts or infer semantic-error counts from a mechanical check.
4. Treat script compliance, exact literal preservation, semantic fidelity and human approval as distinct evidence layers. A glossary risk flag is provisional AI review, not a semantic verdict. Absence of a flag is not approval.
5. Keep recovery-v1 immutable and its completed 20-request allowance closed. Recovery-v2 has separate code, outputs, fixtures and proposals; the master development directive supplies no inference authorization.
6. Keep generated Arabic, AI suggestions and human corrections separate and hash-bound. Human states start pending; a real reviewer must supply their decision, identity, timestamp and attestation. Never fabricate QC.
7. Publish safe aggregate evidence and code on the research branch. Keep private laptop metadata, approval identities and raw result ledgers local. No force push, main changes or Global-MGSM access.
8. Inspect legacy GPU scripts statically because importing them loads models. Prepare masking, leakage and readiness contracts independently; do not start training/export or claim 4 GB feasibility without measurements and authorization.
9. Keep planned experiments and thesis claims explicitly provisional. Small selected diagnostic samples support failure investigation, not an unbiased estimate of corpus-wide quality.
