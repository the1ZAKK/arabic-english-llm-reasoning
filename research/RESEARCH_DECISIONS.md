# Research decisions

1. Preserve frozen English, mathematical literals, recorded ratings, checkpoints and accepted Arabic exactly. Flag source reasoning errors separately; faithful translation must not silently correct them.
2. Count slots, complete fields and complete trajectories separately. Twenty accepted fragments cannot be called twenty complete fields or records without reconstruction.
3. Treat current strict-check failures in historical drafts as revalidation work. Do not rewrite original drafts or infer semantic-error counts from a mechanical check.
4. Treat script compliance, exact literal preservation, semantic fidelity and human approval as distinct evidence layers. A glossary risk flag is provisional AI review, not a semantic verdict. Absence of a flag is not approval.
5. Keep recovery-v1 immutable and its completed 20-request allowance closed. Recovery-v2 has separate code, outputs, fixtures and proposals; the master development directive supplies no inference authorization.
6. Keep generated Arabic, AI suggestions and human corrections separate and hash-bound. Human states start pending; a real reviewer must supply their decision, identity, timestamp and attestation. Never fabricate QC.
7. Publish safe aggregate evidence and code on the research branch. Keep private laptop metadata, approval identities and raw result ledgers local. No force push, main changes or Global-MGSM access.
8. Inspect legacy GPU scripts statically because importing them loads models. Prepare masking, leakage and readiness contracts independently; do not start training/export or claim 4 GB feasibility without measurements and authorization.
9. Keep planned experiments and thesis claims explicitly provisional. Small selected diagnostic samples support failure investigation, not an unbiased estimate of corpus-wide quality.
10. Report strict literal compliance under a named parser version. The inspected English-compound false positive and frozen LaTeX annotation cases show why rejection does not establish a mathematical translation error. Do not change the original parser/plan to remove these failures; any future segmentation change needs separate versioning, source-span provenance and human-reviewed migration design.
11. Deliver the recovery-v2 launcher only as an offline audit/review workflow. It pins tested implementation 2ce19a1 after Windows/Linux CI, leaves the old pilot permanently closed, and never queries Ollama. The 24-request paired diagnostic remains a proposal until actual pilot/design review and separate explicit execution authorization.
12. Treat diagnostic24 as a purposive, clustered comparison of complete policies: seven source problems, five slots from one circle trajectory, different demonstrations and input lengths. Do not claim an isolated context/glossary effect, twelve independent problems or population efficacy. Selected fresh controls are not error-free references. [AI design review](DIAGNOSTIC24_AI_DESIGN_REVIEW_V2_1.md).
13. Separate human design approval from execution consent and translation QC. The bilingual source-only [design package](DIAGNOSTIC24_DESIGN_REVIEW_V2_1.md) starts pending and cannot activate a budget. Prefer different people for design review and blind outcome assessment; disclose prior examples/pilot exposure and conflicts. Preserve both sealed assessments and use independent adjudication; agreement alone approves nothing.
14. Independent adjudication must bind the unchanged comparison, original candidate and exact correction proposal. Require a separately supplied third human identity/attestation and explicit conflict/exposure disclosures; never let a proposer adjudicate their own correction. Retain missing/uncertain cases and AI provenance. Supported corrections remain unapplied, source labels stay frozen and no software report automatically passes the scientific gate. [Workflow](DIAGNOSTIC24_ADJUDICATION_V2_1.md).
