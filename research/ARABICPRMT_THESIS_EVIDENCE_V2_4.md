**Results wording.** In the early draft cohort, filtering to whole mechanically passing trajectories would exclude 224 fields that individually passed, including six train negative steps. We therefore retained blocked groups in the proposed quality-audit frame. We separated mechanical screening, translation fidelity and source-reasoning correctness. The 12-pair comparison remains a proposed development diagnostic; its purposive train-only sample and bundled prompt changes do not support held-out or component-level claims.

**Methods wording.** We planned a stratified audit of source problem groups, keeping train/dev splits and initial whole-trajectory mechanical status fixed. The proposed dev census and within-train random sampling have known inclusion probabilities. We distinguish unresolved-review sensitivity from sampling uncertainty and retain original translations for fidelity assessment. The planned analysis does not substitute a correction for an observed original response.

The next scientific decisions are genuine qualified [diagnostic design review](DIAGNOSTIC24_HUMAN_HANDOFF_V2_4.md), acceptance or revision of the analysis before outcomes, independent reviewer assignments, and a separate owner decision on whether to resource a corpus audit. Any future diagnostic execution requires its own explicit authorization.
