## Inputs and integrity

Supply the exact eight-file comparison directory produced by the existing comparison tool. The adjudicator tool validates both original assessment schemas/seals, frozen sources/candidates/corrections, the exact rebuilt comparison, rendered HTML and original pending planning checklist. Byte checks reject even canonical JSON substitutions such as boolean counters. Extra operator/unblinding files, a changed seal, candidate replacement or a mismatched source block processing without being repaired.

No operator report or A/B-to-arm mapping is read. These are portable bundle-consistency checks; they do not replay the private laptop reservation chain, authenticate a device, establish genuine human identity, or prove absence of prior mapping exposure. No request allowance can come from these records.

## Prepare, review, import

1. Prepare the separate task from the original comparison. All24 candidate judgments, source-concern decisions and correction reviews begin pending; actual identity/time and human/independence attestations are absent.
2. Give the original comparison and the new readable `adjudication.html`, pending JSON and instructions to a third bilingual mathematics reviewer. Original source context, native ratings and Arabic candidates remain visible. Prior assessor judgments are collapsed so the adjudicator can form an initial judgment before inspecting the disagreement evidence.
3. The actual reviewer supplies judgments, explanations, actual identity/UTC time, personal-review and independence attestations, and explicit exposure/conflict disclosures. Upload that real saved record; GPT can validate and seal it without repeated terminal commands by the user. AI must not fill this as completed human review.
4. Import creates a new immutable record/report. Originals remain untouched. Repeated identical inputs verify the existing directory; changed historical records block reuse without overwrite. Input/protected-record races also block processing.

The CLI takes only `--repo-root`, `--comparison` and optional `--adjudication`. It has no execution, budget activation, unblinding, reset, export or training mode. Writes are confined to:

`recovery_artifacts/diagnostic-adjudications-v2.1/<comparison_sha256>/tasks/`

or `.../records/<adjudication_sha256>/`. The existing repository ignore rule keeps these private records out of Git. The old comparison's `adjudication_PENDING.json` remains a planning document and is deliberately incompatible with the new import schema; prepare a fresh task with this tool.

## What can and cannot be decided

Each available candidate receives its own faithful/error/uncertain judgment and explanation. A translation error requires severity/categories. Hard-rejected Arabic/math/number/boundary outputs cannot be promoted to faithful. Missing candidates stay pending/unresolved and remain in the all12-pair/24-output denominator. Partial review remains partial rather than manufacturing completed results.

Each correction decision binds the exact original proposer, assessment and correction hash. Supported proposals are rechecked with the unchanged hard validators and full-field splice validation. The adjudicator must have a distinct normalized recorded identity from both original assessors and correction proposers. A declared conflict blocks independent correction support; retain uncertainty or obtain another reviewer. Prior design/mapping exposure must be disclosed rather than claiming pristine blinding.

Correction support records a supplied independent judgment, **never application to a corpus or original output**. A different correction needs a new proposal and independent review; the adjudicator cannot replace text and approve their own alternative inside this record. Matching proposals or agreeing assessors do not automatically support a correction. The report explicitly counts still-pending/uncertain correction proposals even when all candidate decisions are resolved.

Source concerns have separate decisions and rationale. Native English, ratings, labels and masks remain immutable. The report distinguishes the number of asserted human primary assessments from AI evidence, so a later human adjudication cannot turn two AI reviews into two human primary reviews. Names/attestations remain assertions, not authenticated independence.

Every report retains pending human corpus QC, false training eligibility, zero generation authorization, unapplied corrections and an explicitly unpassed automatic development gate. Genuine human adjudication and source/correction decisions are necessary evidence for later review, not an automatic scientific conclusion, corpus approval or execution authorization.
