Determine whether the contextual glossary policy is promising enough to justify a larger independently reviewed recovery experiment. Fair comparison and bundled intervention / عدالة المقارنة

Both arms use the same source target and identical non-message request settings, schema and hard validators. Control uses the original isolated v1 prompt with a synthetic few-shot example. Contextual v2.1 instead uses full field/problem/adjacent-step context and glossary guidance, including an explicit three-digit wording example. Message count, wording, demonstrations and input length differ. This fairly compares two complete policies under common settings; it does not isolate the causal effect of context or glossary alone, match input tokens, or reproduce historical retry prompts. Twelve new control requests are required; never reuse the exhausted pilot as the control arm.

Predeclared development decision / قرار التطوير المحدد مسبقا

No hard Arabic/math/number/boundary violation may be promoted as faithful. Require human-confirmed faithful contextual outputs for both serious sentinels, resolution of interval/possessive and clear digit agreement problems, and no newly confirmed major or critical contextual regression on fresh controls. Any critical error, missing or unresolved sentinel, unresolved correction or unavailable independent review blocks wider recovery. Passing this qualitative gate only justifies designing a larger reviewed experiment; it is not efficacy, corpus approval or execution authorization. Record descriptive pair outcomes by stratum and source group; do not turn this convenience sample into an accuracy percentage or independent-pair p-value.
