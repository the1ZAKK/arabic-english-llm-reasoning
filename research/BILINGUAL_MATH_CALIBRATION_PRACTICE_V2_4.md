# Independent bilingual mathematical fidelity practice

**Invented practice only.** This sheet contains no diagnostic output, target, policy label, review from another assessor, or answer key. These examples are for discussing the rubric before looking at experimental material. Do not put practice answers into a diagnostic assessment seal.

For **each Arabic candidate independently**, record: faithful, translation error, uncertain, or unavailable; the issue category and severity if applicable; and the source reasoning concern, if any, in a separate field. Explain the smallest concrete difference that matters. Judge the original claim in context, even when the English reasoning is flawed. Keep valid Arabic synonyms and constructions. Do not replace a wrong candidate with a proposed correction.

**بالعربية:** احكم على كل صياغة عربية على حدة: أمينة، خطأ ترجمي، غير محسوم، أو غير متاح. اذكر نوع الخطأ وشدته عند وجوده، وسجّل أي إشكال في الاستدلال الإنجليزي بصورة منفصلة. لا تُصلح الحجة الأصلية أثناء تقييم أمانة الترجمة. لا تُعدّ ممارسة هذه الأمثلة موافقةً على التصميم أو البيانات.

For severity, distinguish a local defect with recoverable meaning from a change to a substantive claim. Reserve critical for a change that reverses or invalidates the task, answer or inference central to the evaluation. Use uncertainty if the supplied context does not settle the judgment.
