## Methodological findings and safe improvements

All twelve selected source slots and their 24 exact requests reproduce. Seven reused slots come from **two of the three prior-pilot source groups**; five share the circle problem and two share the base-number problem. The five selected controls add five distinct problems. All twelve fields are from the training split. This purposive, clustered sample is development evidence, not an independent held-out benchmark or population accuracy estimate.

The arms share target, non-message generation settings and hard validators. The intervention changes demonstrations, wording, context, glossary and prompt length together; an outcome difference cannot identify the effect of one component. Six pairs are control-first and six contextual-first. A fixed seed and temperature zero do not guarantee bitwise generation reproducibility across devices.

Source mathematics must be judged separately. The circle criterion omits the necessary lower-distance bound; squaring an inequality with a negative side is not equivalent. The other contexts include base-representation, disjointness, volume/probability arithmetic and decimal-period concerns. The wrong Circle answer option must not be silently replaced with Sphere. Native ratings, original English and intentional errors stay unchanged. See the [source-bound AI review](DIAGNOSTIC24_AI_HANDOFF_REVIEW_V2_2.md) and original source-only package for the complete evidence.

The supplementary semantic tool now explains a narrow comparison-scope false positive (`and` versus the `or` inside a non-strict comparison) and detects numeral-agreement misses after Arabic clitics. It retains frozen analyses and candidates verbatim; no flag is suppressed or converted into human approval. Separate preparation auditing distinguishes a required digest from an observed installation and a reported history flag from a replayed private ledger. The new [blind-only interface](DIAGNOSTIC24_BLINDED_INTERFACE_V2_2.md) preserves all 24 observations, locks unavailable outputs pending, defaults to AI and saves resumable unsealed drafts with actual metadata/fresh attestations. Python hard validation and immutable sealing remain mandatory.
